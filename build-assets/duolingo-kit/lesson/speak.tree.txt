Notes worth keeping, because they are not obvious from the tree:

  * The wrapper is the same _1fxa4._1Mopf as every other challenge -- only the
    data-test changes. So a speak challenge slots into the existing shell.
  * div._2qg6J > div > canvas is the character. Duolingo PAINTS her into a
    canvas rather than using an <img>, so she cannot be lifted by copying a src.
  * The sentence is one span PER CHARACTER (span._2IGwo.XxgPa), with separate
    absolutely-positioned div[dt=hint-token] overlays laid over the hinted words
    -- the same structure the gapFill prompt uses. Anything reading this prompt
    must walk text nodes rather than trusting innerText.
  * button[aria=Copy this phrase] sits inside the bubble, after the sentence.
  * div._3-hYj holds the microphone button. Its two u_TP- spans are the idle and
    active icon states; img.WXznX is the third.
  * There is NO "can't speak now" control inside the challenge -- that lives in
    the footer as the existing [data-test=player-next], which the player already
    draws. Nothing new to harvest for it.
